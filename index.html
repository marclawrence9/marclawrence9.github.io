Academic Project

AuraGuard AI

A proposed AI-assisted wellness monitoring system for modern educational environments. It explores how camera-based observations, thermal sensing, and pulse-related signals could support wellness screening and trend monitoring.

Proposed Features

  • Camera-based facial and behavioral feature analysis
  • Blink, yawning, and possible fatigue indicators
  • Thermal and PPG sensor data collection concepts
  • AI-assisted risk grouping and wellness trend review
  • Dashboard, data records, and alerts concept

Technology Areas

Python OpenCV MediaPipe ESP32 AI/ML Sensor Integration

E-Barangay

A proposed web-based barangay information and complaint management system designed to help residents access community announcements and submit concerns through a digital platform.

Proposed Features

  • Community announcements and event information
  • Resident complaint and suggestion submissions

  • AI-assisted complaint categorization and priority review
